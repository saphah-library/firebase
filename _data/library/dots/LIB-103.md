TITLE: "Digital Tools for Torah Study: Using Technology Wisely"

## Content

### Overview
Digital tools can significantly enhance Torah study by increasing access to texts, language tools, and community resources. However, technology must remain subordinate to discipleship and not replace direct engagement with Scripture and community learning.

### Core Tool Categories

- **Language Tools**
  Interlinear Bibles, Hebrew lexicons, and morphology resources for original language study.

- **Audio Resources**
  Pronunciation guides, Torah readings, and prayer recordings for auditory learning.

- **Research & Organisation Tools**
  Note-taking systems, reference managers, and structured study databases.

- **Community Platforms**
  Controlled environments for teaching, discussion, and accountability.

### Principles of Responsible Use

- Always verify the reliability and origin of sources
- Avoid dependency on a single application or platform
- Combine digital study with communal reading and teaching
- Maintain offline or backed-up copies of essential resources

### Security and Stewardship

- Protect ministry accounts with strong authentication
- Regularly back up study materials and data
- Limit exposure of sensitive content to uncontrolled environments

### Balance Principle

Technology must serve three functions only:
1. Access to information
2. Organisation of learning
3. Support for teaching and discipleship

It must never replace:
- personal study discipline
- communal instruction
- spiritual discernment

### Conclusion

Used correctly, digital tools expand access to Torah and strengthen study discipline. Used incorrectly, they create dependency and distraction. Wisdom lies in maintaining proper hierarchy between technology and covenant learning.

Baruch YHVH.
