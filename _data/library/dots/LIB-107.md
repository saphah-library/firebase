TITLE: "Messianic Leadership: Principles for Training and Discipleship"

## Content

### Overview
Leadership in Derech Olam is defined by servanthood, Torah fidelity, and the example of Messiah. True leadership is not positional authority but functional responsibility rooted in teaching, accountability, and community formation.

---

### Five Foundational Principles

- **Servanthood**
  Leadership is demonstrated through example, humility, and service rather than coercion or status.

- **Teaching**
  Leaders prioritise Torah, Scripture study, language understanding, and Mo’edim instruction.

- **Accountability**
  Leadership is transparent and subject to correction within the covenant community.

- **Discipleship Multiplication**
  Leaders are trained to produce new leaders, ensuring generational continuity.

- **Spiritual Formation**
  Prayer, fasting, obedience, and personal holiness are essential leadership disciplines.

---

### Practical Training Model

- Short-term apprenticeships under experienced leaders
- Mentorship-based development
- Rotational service roles (teaching, pastoral care, logistics, worship, administration)
- Character evaluation alongside skill assessment

---

### Leadership Structure Goal

The goal is not hierarchy but **reproducible discipleship systems** that ensure:
- continuity of teaching
- integrity of doctrine
- multiplication of capable leaders

---

### Conclusion

Messianic leadership reflects the pattern of Messiah: servant-first, truth-driven, and community-focused. Leadership exists to build others, not to elevate self.

Baruch YHVH.
