TITLE: "The Mo’edim – Appointed Times of YHVH"

## Content

### Overview
This study examines the Mo’edim (appointed times of YHVH), their meaning in Torah, and their prophetic significance within a Paleo-Messianic framework.

### Definition of Mo’edim
The term Mo’edim (מוֹעֲדִים) derives from the root ya’ad, meaning appointed or designated times. These are divine appointments established by YHVH in creation order (Beresith 1:14), marking sacred cycles of time.

### The Spring Mo’edim

- **Pesach (Passover)** — Deliverance through the blood of the Lamb; fulfilled in Yahusha’s sacrifice.
- **Chag HaMatzot (Unleavened Bread)** — Call to remove sin and live in holiness.
- **Yom HaBikkurim (First Fruits)** — Resurrection typology of Yahusha as firstfruits.
- **Shavuot (Weeks)** — Giving of Torah at Sinai and outpouring of the Ruach HaQodesh.

### The Fall Mo’edim

- **Yom Teru’ah (Trumpets)** — Call to repentance and prophetic awakening.
- **Yom Kippur (Atonement)** — Judgment and reconciliation.
- **Sukkot (Tabernacles)** — Dwelling with YHVH and foreshadowing the Messianic Kingdom.

### Prophetic Structure
The Mo’edim function as prophetic rehearsals:
- Spring Mo’edim → First coming of Yahusha
- Fall Mo’edim → Return and kingdom fulfillment

### Scriptural Anchor
Vayikra (Leviticus) 23:4 — The appointed feasts of YHVH.

### Conclusion
The Mo’edim are eternal covenant appointments, not cultural traditions. They reflect the rhythm of YHVH’s calendar and point to the fullness of redemption in Yahusha.
