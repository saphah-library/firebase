TITLE: "Alterations of Derech Olam: From Tree of Life to Tree of Death"

## Content

### Overview
This study explores the concept of Derech Olam (the Way of YHVH) and how it has been altered throughout history through deception, cultural corruption, and religious systems that diverge from the original covenant path.

### The First Alteration – Eden
In Beresith 3, the serpent introduces the first distortion of truth, replacing YHVH’s command with a mixture of truth and deception. This marks the beginning of altered covenant understanding and spiritual deviation.

### Historical Progression of Alteration

- **Babel** — False unity built on rebellion and self-exaltation.
- **Egypt** — System of idolatry and bondage opposing YHVH’s people.
- **Babylonian Exile** — Integration of foreign calendars, traditions, and religious influence.
- **Rabbinic Systems** — Expansion of interpretive fences that obscure original Torah simplicity.
- **Constantinian Era Christianity** — Institutional replacement of Sabbath and appointed times.
- **Modern Religious Systems** — Fragmentation into denominations and doctrinal dilution.

### The Covenant Thread
Despite repeated alteration, the covenant line remains intact through the remnant: Noach, Avraham, Mosheh, the Prophets, and Yahusha. The Tree of Life remains accessible to those who return in humility and obedience.

### Scriptural Anchor
Devarim 30:15–19 — The choice between life and death, blessing and curse.

### Our Call Today
Believers are called to return to covenant alignment, test teachings, restore appointed times, and walk in obedience to YHVH through Yahusha.

### Conclusion
The Way of YHVH remains unchanged. Alterations are human deviations, not divine changes. Restoration is a return, not an invention.

Baruch YHVH.
