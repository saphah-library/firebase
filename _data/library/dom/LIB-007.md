TITLE: "The Covenant Thread: From Genesis to Revelation"

## Content

### Overview
This study traces the unified covenant thread throughout Scripture, showing continuity from Genesis (Beresith) to Revelation as one progressive divine plan of redemption.

### The First Promise – Eden
After the fall, YHVH declares the promise of the Seed (Beresith 3:15), understood as the first covenantal prophecy pointing toward Messiah Yahusha.

### Major Covenant Stages

- **Noachic Covenant** — Preservation of life and creation through the flood.
- **Avrahamic Covenant** — Promise of land, seed, and global blessing.
- **Mosaic Covenant** — Torah given as instruction for righteous living.
- **Davidic Covenant** — Eternal kingship established through David’s line.
- **Renewed Covenant** — Torah written on the heart through Messiah Yahusha.

### Covenant Unity Principle
These covenants are not separate systems but layers of a single unfolding plan. Each expands the previous revelation without replacing it.

### Scriptural Anchor
2 Corinthians 1:20 — All promises find their fulfillment in Messiah.

### The Covenant in Messiah
Yahusha is the central fulfillment of the covenant thread. He does not abolish Torah but internalises it, bringing covenant alignment into the heart of the believer.

### Conclusion
The Bible is a unified covenant narrative. From Eden to the New Creation, YHVH’s plan remains consistent, revealing His character through promise, fulfillment, and restoration.
