TITLE: "The Tabernacle Pattern: YHVH’s Dwelling Among His People"

## Content

### Overview
This study explores the Mishkan (Tabernacle) as the divinely designed dwelling place of YHVH among Israel, revealing patterns of worship, holiness, and Messianic fulfillment.

### A Heavenly Pattern
The Tabernacle was not human invention but a revealed pattern shown to Mosheh on Mount Sinai (Shemot 25:40). Every component reflects divine order and instruction.

### Structure of the Tabernacle

- **Outer Court** — Place of sacrifice and cleansing; entry point through atonement.
- **Holy Place** — Contains the menorah, table of showbread, and altar of incense, symbolising light, provision, and intercession.
- **Most Holy Place** — Dwelling of YHVH’s presence above the Ark of the Covenant.

This structure represents progression from cleansing → fellowship → divine presence.

### Messianic Fulfillment
Yahusha fulfills the Tabernacle pattern:
- Sacrifice — atonement for sin
- Bread of Life — spiritual sustenance
- Light of the World — illumination
- Intercessor — access to the Father

The tearing of the veil signifies restored access to the Most Holy Place.

### Believers as Living Temples
Scripture reveals that believers now serve as the dwelling place of YHVH’s Spirit (1 Corinthians 3:16). This extends the Tabernacle pattern into personal and communal life.

### Scriptural Anchor
Yohanan 1:14 — The Word became flesh and tabernacled among us.

### Conclusion
The Tabernacle is both historical blueprint and prophetic revelation, pointing to Messiah and defining the believer’s identity as a living temple set apart for YHVH.

Baruch YHVH.
